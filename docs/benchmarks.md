# Comparison sources

Reviewed 8 November 2026. These are playful reference targets, not population percentiles or medical assessments. Cards show the comparison group; details retain the method and limitations. Goals rounded upward still exceed the cited mean. Scores and evidence are self-reported.

Running compares averages of outings, rather than a personal best. Bench press uses relative load, not absolute kilograms. Plank, balance and reach use the stated test form. Musical listening uses the Mini-PROMS raw score (which permits half points), rather than a percentile or an instrument-performance claim. Standardized external tests open on their authors’ sites; the app records results.

## Rank-data cards

- **CS2 Premier: 10,000+.** [Leetify’s September 2024 table](https://leetify.com/blog/cs2-ranks/) sums to 2,195,148 sampled accounts. Of these, 1,193,742 (about 54%) fall below 10,000. This is an upper-half target from a historical distribution, not a current mean or live percentile. Seasonal recalibration limits comparisons across years.
- **League Solo/Duo: Gold II+.** [League of Graphs](https://www.leagueofgraphs.com/rankings/rank-distribution), all regions, patch 16.22, retrieved 8 November 2026. Cumulative rounded shares were 48.37% through Gold IV and 54.27% through Gold III. Gold III contains the median; Gold II is the first whole division clearly above it. Do not infer an exact Soloqueue sample size from the site’s overall player count.

Both datasets describe observed ranked accounts, including possible alternate accounts. Rank ordering is explicit in the League dropdown; source links may show newer distributions after the snapshot date. Refresh data and dates together when updating these targets.

## Kept as personal targets

Playing a whole song, metronome practice and the browser reaction game remain personal targets. They offer useful goals without inventing a study average. Users can customize any sourced card into a personal goal; its citation is then removed so the edited rule is not attributed to the study.
