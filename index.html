What does “average” mean?

Study-based challenges use published results to set a target. Rank cards use a dated player distribution. Each card names the group you’re comparing with; some targets are rounded to make them easier to aim for.

These are useful reference points, rather than a ranking against everyone on Earth. Personal targets are goals you set for yourself. Results and evidence are self-reported.
